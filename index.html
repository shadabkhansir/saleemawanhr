About

HR that's built to scale

I'm an HR Business Partner with CHRMP and SHRM-CP certifications and over 13 years of experience in retail, hospitality, restaurants, construction and FMCG.

Much of my career has been spent setting up HR where there was none. I was the first HR hire at Bundu Khan Foods and part of the pre-opening team at Defence Raya Golf & Country Club, where I built the function from an initial hiring drive of 400 people to a team managing more than 800 employees.

Today I run HR operations for Nazih Group in Dubai, from payroll and recruitment to performance management, employee relations and all government and visa processes with MOHRE, GDRFA and DED.
